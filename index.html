Questions

Frequently asked questions

What sizes do you make?

Two sizes. Rectangles are 2.5" x 3.5" and can be tall or wide. Squares are 2.5" x 2.5".

How long will my order take?

Your magnets are ready within 3 days of your order. If you chose shipping, I mail them right after by USPS. If you chose pickup, I'll email you to set a time.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is free on orders of $50 or more. Smaller orders ship for a flat $7, no matter how many magnets. I ship anywhere in the US. Pickup in Cary, NC is always free.

What kind of photos work best?

Clear, well-lit photos in JPEG or PNG format. Upload the original photo from your phone or camera if you can. Screenshots and photos saved from text messages are often too small and can print blurry.

Can I crop the photo myself?

Yes. When you upload a photo you can zoom, move and rotate it to fit the magnet. The edge of the photo wraps around the sides, so the shop shows you where to keep faces. If you'd rather not, choose "Let me crop it for you" and I'll do it.

How sturdy are the magnets?

Each magnet is pressed by hand on a rigid base, just under 3/16" thick. They're made to hold up on the fridge, a locker or a filing cabinet.

Do you make magnets for businesses?

Yes. I make logo and marketing magnets for local businesses, realtors and contractors, with no minimum order. Send me a message and I'll get back to you with pricing.

Can you press magnets live at my event?

Yes. I can set up and press magnets for your guests on the spot, or make favor magnets ahead of time. Tell me your date and what you have in mind.

When should I order for Christmas?

Please place Christmas orders by Tuesday, December 15.

Do you charge sales tax?

NC sales tax is added at checkout for orders shipped to North Carolina and for Cary pickups.

What if something's wrong with my order?

Email me at scott@themagnetfairy.com and I'll work with you to make it right.
